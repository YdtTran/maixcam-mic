## Decision and remaining checks

Do not adopt SCO yet. Keep production A2DP. No connector restart, receiver
change, Bluetooth lifecycle change, PC restart, video or recording change.
No persistent SCO and no duplex.

Actual A2DP browser speech baseline, repeated browser push-to-talk comparison,
first-syllable clipping, audible return recovery and common-recording acoustic
latency remain pending. The isolated continuous-audio gate failed, so no live
SCO route trial was performed. Guide specifies 20 cycles per profile, a long
hold, A2DP repeat, rollback and recording method for subsequent validation.

No measured latency improvement. Avoiding A2DP resampling/transport changes is
a potential benefit only. The next narrow investigation should separate PCM
buffering/draining, SCO transport and test-feeding behavior before changing
buffer settings or connection lifecycle.
